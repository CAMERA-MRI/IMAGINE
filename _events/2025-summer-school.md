The **2025 IMAGINE Summer School** kicked off with a hands-on pre-congress workshop at the **ESMRMB 2025 Conference** in Vienna, Austria. 

Trainees from Africa, Europe, and North America gathered to perform the first live, on-site assembly of the **ERNIE 1** scanner. The session focused on the fundamentals of low-field MRI physics, design thinking strategies, and practical training in assembling Halbach arrays and circuit boards.
