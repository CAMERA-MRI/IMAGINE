The **2026 IMAGINE Summer School** was held in Cape Town, South Africa, aligned with the **ISMRM 2026 Conference**. 

During this event, the **ERNIE 1** scanner was re-assembled and calibrated, and its field homogeneity was mapped using the completed field mapping robot. The workshop successfully demonstrated that two independent sites (in Uganda/South Africa and Montreal) can build identical, high-performance educational scanners using identical open hardware parts, proving the reproducibility of the ERNIE design.
