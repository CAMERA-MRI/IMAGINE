Under the leadership of researcher **Maureen Nayebare**, a series of hands-on educational workshops were conducted across several major biomedical research institutions in Montreal.

These sessions focused on the construction and testing of the **ERNIE 2** kit. Over several weeks, students and engineers at the **Montreal Neurological Institute (The Neuro)**, **Montreal General Hospital**, **Polytechnique Montréal**, and the **Douglas Mental Health University Institute** successfully assembled the 7-ring magnet array and calibrated the field mapping robot. The workshops provided local students with direct exposure to low-field MRI engineering, system integration, and hardware troubleshooting.
