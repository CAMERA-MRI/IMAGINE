\f1\b0 \
4mm Threaded brass rod 262 mm long. 8 of these rods are used to fasten the rings together, structurally connect them and provide the means to create the necessary spacing between the rings.\

\f0\b Lid_Loose.ipt:
\f1\b0 \
Lid used specifically for Rings 842, 945 and 1050. This lid has 13 3.5mm holes to accommodate the M3 screws.\'a0 These lids need to be made to prevent magnets from falling out of the ring housings. 5 of these lids need to be made for the following ring housings: \'a01 x Ring 842, 2x Ring 946 and 2x Ring 1050\

\f0\b Lid_tight.ipt:
\f1\b0 \
Lid used specifically for Rings 0, 1 and 10. This lid has 10 3.5mm holes to accommodate the M3 screws. 6 of these lids need to be made to prevent magnets from falling out of the ring housings. 6 of these rings need to be made for the following ring housings: 2x Ring 0, 2x Ring 1 and 2x Ring 10.\

\f0\b M3x8.ipt:
\f1\b0 \
A 3mm diameter 8mm long screw. These are used to fasten the lids to the ring housings. A total of 125 screws are required to fasten all the lids.\

\f0\b M4 Dome nut.ipt:
\f1\b0 \
These nuts act as the fasteners for the brass rod ends which protrude out on either end of the assembled magnet rings. The dome nuts are fastened on the back end of Ring_10_Back and the front of Ring_10. A total of 16 half dome nuts is required to fasten both ends.\

\f0\b M4_Nylon_Washer.ipt:
\f1\b0 \
Washers that are used anytime when a fastener (nut) is used to fasten against a ring housing. The washers should be placed between the ring housing and the fastener.\

\f0\b M4x3.2mm Hex Nut.ipt:
\f1\b0 \
M4 nuts used to hold the ring housings in place along the brass rods. These nuts are used to determine the spacing between rings on the brass rod and ensure they remain in place at those positions. The nuts are placed on either side of the ring housing (unless they are the rings on the ends (Ring_10). A total of 160 hex nuts are used.\
