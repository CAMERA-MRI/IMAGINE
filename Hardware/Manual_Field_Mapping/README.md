# Manual Magnetic Field Mapper for the IMAGINE Rat MRI Scanner

## Overview

The manual magnetic field mapper is a low-cost, manually operated system for measuring the static magnetic field generated by the Halbach array in the IMAGINE rat MRI scanner. It provides a simple approach to collecting field measurements at selected positions within the scanner bore without requiring a motorized positioning system.

## Scanner and Mapper Specifications

| Parameter | Description |
|---|---|
| Scanner | IMAGINE low-field Halbach array rat MRI scanner |
| Scanner inner bore diameter | 120 mm |
| Diameter of spherical volume (DSV) | 60 mm |
| Mapper grid diameter | 119 mm |
| Measurement regions | Points within a 60 mm or 119 mm diameter circle |
| Bore direction | Z-axis |
| Transverse measurement planes | XY planes |
| Example axial step size | 10 mm (user-adjustable) |

**Note:** The 60 mm DSV is the scanner's specified field-of-interest region. Measurements across the larger 119 mm diameter circle can be used for more extensive field characterization, but they should not be interpreted as measurements limited to the 60 mm DSV.

## Working Principle

The field mapper uses a grid-based holder to position a magnetic field sensor at selected locations in an XY plane. The mapper assembly is manually translated along the scanner bore (Z-axis) by a chosen step size, such as 10 mm. At each Z position, the sensor is moved to the desired XY grid locations, and the magnetic field is recorded. Repeating these measurements across successive XY planes produces a three-dimensional field dataset.

## Assembly and Alignment

1. Connect the manual field mapper to its positioning adjuster.
2. Install the magnetic field sensor securely in the sensor holder.
3. Place the mapper in the 120 mm inner bore of the rat scanner.
4. Adjust the mapper so that its reference position is aligned with the center of the scanner's 60 mm DSV.
5. Check that the mapper and sensor can move safely without touching the magnet bore or other components.

## Field Mapping Procedure

1. **Select the measurement region:** Choose grid points within either the 60 mm diameter circle or the larger 119 mm diameter circle, depending on the purpose of the measurement.
2. **Set the axial step size:** Choose the spacing between XY measurement planes along the Z-axis (for example, 10 mm).
3. **Measure the first XY plane:** Position the sensor at each selected grid location and record its X and Y coordinates, Z position, and magnetic field reading.
4. **Move along the bore:** Translate the mapper manually along Z by the selected step size.
5. **Repeat the XY measurements:** Measure the same selected grid points at the new Z position.
6. **Complete the mapping region:** Continue until all desired Z positions have been measured.
7. **Save and analyze the data:** Compile the coordinates and field readings to visualize the field distribution and assess field homogeneity over the chosen measurement volume.

## Suggested Data Format

Record each measurement in a CSV file with columns such as `x_mm`, `y_mm`, `z_mm`, and `field_reading`.

Specify the magnetic field unit and whether the instrument reports field magnitude or a particular field component before analyzing the data.

## Practical Notes

- The 119 mm mapper grid is nearly as large as the 120 mm scanner bore, so verify mechanical clearances carefully before inserting or moving the assembly.
- Use a consistent coordinate origin and sensor orientation throughout the measurements.
- Record the actual Z step size and the positions measured in each XY plane.
