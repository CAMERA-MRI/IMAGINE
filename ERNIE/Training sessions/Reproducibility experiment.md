**Reproducibility experiment**
<p style="text-align: justify;">
To evaluate the reproducibility of the ERNIE toolkit, we conducted multi-site reconstruction experiments in which independent teams rebuilt the scanner at geographically distinct locations using similar documentation and design package, including training package, instructions and kit. The primary objective was to assess whether the toolkit enables consistent system reconstruction and functional performance regardless of change in site.
</p> 

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  Reproducibility was assessed across 3 different dimensions: (i) Assembly reproducibility, (ii) operational reproducibility and (iii) consistency in field map results. 
</p> 

<p style="text-align: justify;">

**Outcome**
 
All participating sites successfully reconstructed the scanner and robot using the same ERNIE kit. These sites included ESMRMB (ERNIE 1), The Neuro (ERNIE 2), Polytechnique Montreal(ERNIE 2), Montreal General Hospital (ERNIE 2), The Douglas (ERNIE 2) and ISMRM (ERNIE1 & ERNIE2). 
</p>
