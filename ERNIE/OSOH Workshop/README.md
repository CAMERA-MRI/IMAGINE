## 🧪🌸 Open Science Office Hours (OSOH) Educational Session  

**Date:** March 13, 2026  
**Venue:** de Grandpré Communications Centre, Montreal Neurological Institute, McGill University
In preparation for the session, participants were recruited in collaboration with the Open Science Office Hours initiative, drawing individuals from diverse backgrounds including neuroscience, biomedical engineering, and related disciplines. A total of 35 participants took part in the session, with approximately 50% assigned to the robotic assembly team and 45% to the magnet assembly team.

The session was conducted from 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM. Participants were organized into four groups; the first two groups concurrently engaged in magnet and robotic assembly tasks. After a 30-minute interval, the remaining groups rotated through the same build activities.

Following the assembly phase, all teams reconvened to perform magnetic field mapping of the scanner. A total of 500 spatial points were sampled using a robotic field mapping system, and the resulting field distribution was analyzed and visualized. A diameter spherical volume (DSV) homogeneity of approximately 8,000 ppm was achieved.
