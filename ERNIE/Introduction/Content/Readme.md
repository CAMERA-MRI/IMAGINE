**Training  Sessions**
<p style="text-align: justify;">
The toolkit (ERNIE) was implemented in October 2025 at the European Society of Magnetic Resonance in Medicine and Biology (EMSMRB) CAMERA Pre-Congress workshop to pilot its use as a self-sustaining OS MRI education resource for settings that do not have well-equipped LF lab training spaces. A second  toolkit (ERNIE 2) was developed to evaluate the kit’s reproducibility before creating the kit’s free online tutorial with the concepts lecture, animated assembly guides, and field mapping test software. ERNIE is currently being assembled, and its reproducibility and MRI engineering pedagogy was evaluated by 200 students and researchers across 5 events from March to May 2026, including at The Neuro (Open Science), Polytechnique Montreal, The Douglas Mental Hospital, Montreal General Hospital, McGill Library, Montreal Public Library, and at the International Society of Magnetic Resonance in Medicine (ISMRM) conference in Cape Town, South Africa, 2026. 
</p>
