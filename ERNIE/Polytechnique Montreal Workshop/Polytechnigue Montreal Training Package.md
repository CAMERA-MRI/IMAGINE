

Hands-on workshop on building a Simple Low-Field MRI Scanner Training Package

Overview: This workshop is organized by the Low-field Laboratory at the McConnell Brain Imaging Center of the Montreal Neurological Institute in collaboration with the Imaging Without Borders (IMAGINE) Summer School. In this interactive 2-hour workshop you will learn the basics of brain magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) hardware. You will assemble a simple MRI scanner, and then measure and characterize its magnetic field using a 3D field-mapping robot that you will build at the workshop. 

Prerequisites: No prior hardware experience is needed. Your curiosity and a willingness to learn is all that is required. 

Code of Conduct: The workshop is a harassment-free and safe environment for all, regardless of race, cultural affiliation, gender, nationality, beliefs, age, or disability. All participants are expected to comply with this code of conduct, and violations of its standards may result in dismissal from the workshop at the discretion of the workshop organizers. Upholding the standards of behavior is mandatory to maintain the workshop’s integrity and create a positive environment. All participants are expected to behave professionally and responsibly towards everyone during all activities of the workshop, regardless of the session.

Safety Instructions: The workshop provides a hands-on introduction to low-field MRI engineering through the assembly and testing of an open-source educational scanner. Participants will work with basic tools, strong neodymium magnets, and simple electrical components in a guided environment. The neodymium magnets can have strong magnetic fields and can cause pinching injuries, damage electronic devices, or be damaged if not handled with caution. You may also work with electrical connections, do not touch any electrical components or connections, unless instructed and under supervision.

Workshop Activities: Over the course of the workshop, you will engage in: 
Provide a Photo Use Consent 
Complete a Pre-training Survey to help improve the workshop for future participants 
Review safety and code of conduct agreements.
Participate in a Lecture on the Fundamentals of MRI Hardware for low-field brain imaging. 
Work together to assemble a low-field MRI scanner and an MRI field-mapping system. 
Assess the field of the low-field MRI scanner you build to see if it provides a good basis to scan brain images at a very low magnetic field. 
Complete a Post-training Survey to help improve the workshop for future participants.
Participate in a Group photo to commemorate this fun experience
