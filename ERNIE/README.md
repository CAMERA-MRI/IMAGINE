We introduce the CAMERA-IMAGINE- MRI Education (ERNIE) toolkit, designed to extend this hands-on collaborative model through a train-the-trainer approach. ERNIE is a frugal educational toolkit for on-site construction of a 50 mT OS-LF ‘mice-like’ brain MRI scanner that can be readily reproduced in any setting using common assembly tools and without prior MRI engineering experience. The toolkit was designed as a reusable teaching resource consists of:</p>

   1) a 30-minute lecture on OS-LF MRI concepts
   2) an animation of step-by-step guide of scanner and robot assembly and field mapping (quality assurance)
   3) 3D printing instructions and bill of materials for the magnet and robotic field mapping parts.
   4) tools for onsite assembly.
   5) Instructions and software to map the field’s homogeneity.
   6) The magnet is a 7-ring, 80mm Halbach array producing ~50 mT and made from 99 neodymium (N48) magnets.
