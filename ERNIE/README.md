We introduce the ESMRMB-IMAGINE- MRI Education (ERNIE) toolkit, designed to extend this hands-on collaborative model through a train-the-trainer approach. ERNIE is a **frugal educational toolkit** for on-site construction of a 50 mT OS-LF ‘mice-like’ brain MRI scanner that can be readily reproduced in any setting using common assembly tools and without prior MRI engineering experience. The toolkit was designed as a reusable teaching resource consists of:
1) a 30-minute lecture on OS-LF MRI concepts
2) an animation of step-by-step guide of scanner and robot assembly and field mapping (quality assurance)
3) 3D printing instructions and bill of materials for the magnet and robotic field mapping parts.
4) tools for onsite assembly.
5) Instructions and software to map the field’s homogeneity.
6) The magnet is a 7-ring, 80mm Halbach array producing ~50 mT and made from 99 neodymium (N48) magnets. Rings and lids are 3D printed and digitally prototyped in Autodesk Inventor. A Genetic Algorithm was used for design optimization. The robot is a 3-axis Cartesian positioner with a GM2 Gaussmeter (Alpha Labs, DC Milligauss Meter Model MGM) as an end effector, capable of automatically mapping the B₀ field. It uses three stepper motors, TB6600 drivers, and an Arduino UNO R4 Minima. Each motor moves along a short linear stage. The toolkit also includes all tools, accessories, and software required for magnet and robot assembly for a team of learners.
