
## Gradient Power Amplifier (GPA)

The IMAGINE/ERNIE scanner (Canada's site) uses the open-source [FHDO Gradient Power Amplifier (GPA-FHDO)](https://github.com/menkueclab/GPA-FHDO).

For detailed information on the amplifier design, assembly, operation, and testing procedures, please refer to the [original GPA-FHDO documentation](https://gpa-fhdo.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).

The amplifier design follows the original FHDO implementation, with selected components modified or substituted to meet the requirements of the IMAGINE scanner. These changes are documented in the updated Bill of Materials (BOM).
