IMAGINE Project

An open-source preclinical MRI initiative of the Consortium for Advancement of MRI Education and Research in Africa (CAMERA). Sustaining diagnostic health equity globally.

Open Science Compliance

All hardware files are released under open-source hardware licenses. This project is developed to satisfy the requirements of the Open Source Imaging Initiative (OSI²).
