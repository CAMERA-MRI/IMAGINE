Open Science MRI

IMAGINE: Imaging Without Borders

Building a low-cost, open-source low-field preclinical MR scanner to sustainably democratize diagnostic imaging and biomedical training across the globe.

Explore Scanners Upcoming Workshops

1. Open Hardware

Fully open designs, standard CAD/BOM tables, and compliance with the OSI² standard, allowing researchers and students to modify, edit, and build scanners from scratch.

2. Capacity Building

Through training schools like the CAMERA summer school and local workshops, we train next-generation engineers and physicists to collaboratively grow and share knowledge.

3. Frugal Innovation

Using consumer electronics (RedPitaya), low-cost amplifiers, and 3D printed formers, we make a functional preclinical MRI scanner costing thousands instead of millions.

Project Philosophy

Over 70% of the world's population lacks access to clinical magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) systems. Standard clinical systems are highly expensive, heavy, require liquid helium cooling, and need massive infrastructure to run. The **IMAGINE** project, a training initiative of the **Consortium for Advancement of MRI Education and Research in Africa (CAMERA)**, addresses this disparity.

Our goal is to design preclinical scanners that embody ease of use, safety, and adaptability. We implement RAD-AID’s **Teach-Try-Use** strategy, providing engineers, physicists, and clinicians with design thinking competencies to build, maintain, and adapt their own scanners locally.
