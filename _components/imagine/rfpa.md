  #### RFPA Gain and Compression Verification
  1. **WARNING**: Always connect a 50 Ohm dummy load to the RFPA output before powering on. Never run the amplifier open-circuit!
  2. Connect a low-power signal generator to the RFPA input.
  3. Measure the output amplitude on an oscilloscope (attenuated!) or power meter.
  4. Verify that the gain is flat around the 2 MHz Larmor frequency and that output power meets the required peak specifications (e.g. 50-100W peak RF).

The **RF Power Amplifier (RFPA)** boosts the weak RF pulses generated by the Red Pitaya console (STEMlab) to the high-power levels (tens of watts) required to rotate the nuclear spins in the preclinical sample. It is enclosed in a shielded aluminum box to prevent RF leakage and powered by a low-noise linear power supply to avoid switching noise.
